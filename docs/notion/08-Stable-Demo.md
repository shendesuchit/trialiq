# Stable Demo

## Demo ladder

TrialIQ qualifies four levels against the currently loaded full graph.

### Basic

Direct evidence-grounded study lookup. Demonstrates that TrialIQ does not force agentic complexity where it is unnecessary.

### Intermediate

A small 2–6 study related-trial set through one clear relationship type. Demonstrates governed discovery without HITL.

### Advanced

A 2–6 study set with strong deterministic comparison coverage in at least two dimensions among completion, duration and enrollment.

### HITL

A broad result set of seven or more candidates. Demonstrates pause, investigator selection and resume without repeated retrieval.
